Prioritize pathways beyond the familiar literature

Interpretation boundary Low literature saturation identifies an under-examined pathway, gene-panel, and disease intersection. It is a prioritization signal, not proof of biological novelty.

Analytical workflow

From panel to testable hypothesis

  1. Validate inputs. Normalize gene symbols and record the disease context.
  2. Expand the network. Retrieve high-confidence STRING interaction partners.
  3. Resolve pathways. Enrich across Reactome, KEGG, WikiPathways, and Gene Ontology.
  4. Rank the evidence. Combine significance, effect size, specificity, and replication.
  5. Measure saturation. Count the exact PubMed pathway, seed, and disease corpus.
  6. Audit each result. Review lineage, expression, permutation statistics, and citations.
