How discovery works

  1. Seed genes — your validated input.
  2. Functional neighborhood — STRING interaction partners.
  3. Primary pathways — enrichment, with pathways already known for the seeds filtered out.
  4. Secondary expansion — literature-mined genes open up unreported pathways.
  5. Scoring & filtering — NES score + cardiac semantic relevance.
  6. Evidence — PubMed citations + seed-gene lineage for every hypothesis.
