Documentation

CardioXNet reasons outward from a focused seed panel to auditable cardiovascular pathway hypotheses and literature-sparse query intersections. This page explains the pipeline, how to run an analysis, and how to read every result without treating a narrow query gap as proof of globally unreported biology.

The known → unknown pipeline

  1. Seed validation. Your gene symbols are mapped to NCBI Entrez identifiers.
  2. Functional neighborhood. High-confidence STRING interaction partners are assembled around the seeds.
  3. Primary pathways. Enrichment (g:Profiler over GO:BP / KEGG / Reactome / WikiPathways) identifies enriched pathways. Pathways already annotated to the seeds in Reactome are filtered to focus the expansion step.
  4. Secondary expansion. Primary-pathway member genes are re-enriched. Literature-derived genes can optionally augment the highest-priority primary pathways before secondary enrichment.
  5. Aggregation & scoring. Pathway instances are deduplicated and combined (Fisher's method). Base PPS combines statistical significance (0.30), fold enrichment (0.30), gene-set specificity (0.25), and upstream support (0.15), scaled by database weight.
  6. Cardiac filtering. A bounded lexical cardiovascular-relevance multiplier adjusts PPS before progressive retention. It is an inspectable heuristic, not a validated biological classifier.
  7. Validation. A size-matched permutation null over the protein-coding genome with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control, GTEx v8 cardiac tissue expression, and curated druggability annotate each surviving hypothesis.
  8. Evidence. Comprehensive PubMed mining attaches ranked citations, and each hypothesis is traced back through its full seed → primary → secondary → final lineage.
  9. Topology. A network analysis highlights hub genes and prioritized druggable therapeutic targets.

A full methods description, scoring formulas, and limitations are in docs/METHODS.md.

Running an analysis

  1. Enter seed genes on the Discover page (space-, comma-, or newline-separated). Use Validate genes to confirm they map to known symbols.
  2. Pick a disease context (optional) to focus semantic and literature scoring - e.g. heart failure or diabetic cardiomyopathy.
  3. Choose analysis depth:
    • Fast screening (~5 min) - quick triage. Skips the slow live GTEx and Open Targets calls and the permutation null, substituting fast offline curated estimates for the tissue-specificity and druggability columns.
    • Balanced (~10 min) - recommended default; full live validation.
    • Comprehensive (~20 min) - deepest expansion, literature recall, and validation.
    Advanced parameters (STRING neighbors, FDR threshold, PubMed depth, permutations) can override any preset.
  4. Start discovery and watch the live stage timeline. You'll be taken to the results automatically.

Results columns at a glance

Every column in the pathway hypotheses table, and what it measures.

ColumnMeaning
DiscoveryDiscovery Score is the primary ranking. It multiplies PPS by composite novelty, weighted 2:1 toward exact-query literature novelty over low seed overlap. High values identify supported, comparatively under-examined intersections; they do not prove biological novelty.
Literature tierOperational tier informed by the strict pathway × seed genes × disease query and seed overlap: Highly Novel, Novel, Emerging, or Established. Unverified means the strict query or a required seed-corpus or concept-corpus prerequisite was unavailable. Ungrounded means the seed genes have too little disease literature for a tier to be meaningful. Both use a conservative scoring corpus and are excluded from verified literature-sparse counts.
PPSPathway Prioritization Score is the base weighted composite of significance, fold enrichment, gene-set specificity, and upstream support, scaled by database weight, then adjusted by the bounded semantic cardiovascular-relevance multiplier. Network topology is reported separately. It is not a GSEA normalized enrichment score.
Perm. qBenjamini–Hochberg corrected empirical p-value from a size-matched permutation null. n/t means the null could not be built. Most enriched pathways clear this null comfortably, so it confirms an overlap is not chance rather than ranking among strong hypotheses.
Combined p*Fisher upper-tail probability computed from per-query g:Profiler adjusted enrichment values. Upstream expansion branches can overlap, so this is a ranking input rather than an independent confirmatory test.
GenesNumber of evidence genes supporting the pathway hypothesis.
Cardiac rel.Semantic cardiovascular relevance of the pathway (0–100%), focused by your disease context.
Cardiac expr.The share of the pathway's genes expressed in cardiac tissue (GTEx Heart - Left Ventricle + Atrial Appendage, median TPM ≥ 1). A fast curated estimate is used when live validation is off. Per-gene cardiac specificity (heart vs. other tissues) is shown on each pathway's detail page.
DruggabilityTherapeutic tractability tier (high / medium / low) from curated DrugBank / ChEMBL / TTD target sets, refined with live Open Targets evidence when enabled.
Lit.Count of supporting PubMed citations attached to the hypothesis.
Traced seedsWhich of your seed genes this hypothesis was reached from during expansion.
seed-independentFlagged when seed genes are a minority (<50%) of the pathway evidence. This is a structural expansion property and makes no claim about the literature.

Reading the results

  • Summary tiles separate literature-sparse hypotheses, seed-independent pathways, secondary expansion, and statistical support.
  • Pathway table - click any column header to re-sort by Discovery Score, PPS, adjusted p-value, permutation q, fold enrichment, gene count, cardiac relevance, tissue specificity, or citation count. Click a row to open its detail page.
  • Pathway detail - discovery lineage, statistics (permutation-based empirical p-values, cardiac expression and specificity), evidence genes, and ranked PubMed citations with abstracts.
  • Network & targets - hub genes and prioritized druggable targets.
  • Export - download the full table as CSV, or the report as PDF / Markdown / JSON.

Interpreting the seed-independent flag

A hypothesis is flagged seed-independent when the seed genes account for fewer than half of the pathway evidence genes. This means expansion carries most of the overlap; it does not mean the connection is unreported. Use the separate exact-query count, literature tier, grounding state, and Discovery Score to assess literature sparsity, then confirm the query with synonyms and expert review.
